# Product

> **Status: current.** Describes what we are building and why.

## The rule the whole system exists to enforce

> If a person or vehicle is not registered, approved and verified through the
> accreditation system, they do not enter the operational areas of the venue.

Salman's brief, 29 Aug 2026.

## What happens today

Crew accreditation runs on WhatsApp and a BookMyShow dashboard. Names arrive in
messages, someone types them into BMS, tickets are issued, and at the venue there
is no reliable way to tell who has already collected a wristband. The brief opens
with *"It's always chaotic in India"* and points at the Wacken (W:O:A) system as
the model.

The Wacken voucher we were sent confirms the shape we are copying: one voucher per
person carrying their name, company (*Firma*), sub-company and team; a check-in
desk at a fixed address with published opening hours; **the voucher plus a valid
photo ID**; and no collection on anyone else's behalf.

## The seven rules

1. Every person working at BOA is registered **individually**.
2. Approved names are entered into BMS by Bhoopesh and a ticket issued. **This is
   manual. There is no BMS API.** The app tracks who is still to be entered.
3. Each person brings their own pass to the Crew Check-In desk.
4. **Every single person checks in physically.** One person = one pass = one
   physical check-in = one wristband.
5. Wristbands cannot be collected on behalf of anyone else.
6. Vehicles are registered to a plate and carry a sticker bound to that plate.
   **No vehicle enters without the sticker**, and security checks sticker **and**
   wristband — both, or neither.
7. **Artists are the exception to all of it.** Their accreditation is handled at
   their hotel and never touches this system.

## Three bands, no fourth

| Type | Band | Zones | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working Crew | Poison green `#00F5A5` | Site · Backstage · Prod office · Bar | most people working the festival |
| Stage | Ritual gold `#E3BB72` | Site · Backstage · Stage · Prod office | only people working on or around the stages |
| AAA | Bone white `#F2EFE2` | all seven | named list only, approved by Salman |

Type is assigned **at approval** and sets the colour and the zones. Serials are
typed in **at the gate** as bands are handed over, never pre-assigned. AAA is not
requestable on the vendor form.

## Three audiences

| Who | Needs | Gets |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor company contact | to list their crew without an account | a secret link, a quota bar, and after approval their crew's passes to distribute |
| Accreditation lead (Bhoopesh) | to approve 641 people and know what is left | the Control room, the queue, the master list, the BMS worklist |
| Gate volunteer | to answer one question in three seconds, with no signal | green / amber / red, and a band to hand over |

## The three numbers that matter on show day

- How many are approved but have not collected.
- How many are approved but not yet in BMS.
- Whether this person, in front of me, has already been given a band.
