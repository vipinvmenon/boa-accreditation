Control room

The numbers: bands still to collect, not in BookMyShow, vehicle stickers, the AAA list, and who came through the gates today. Every tile opens the matching list.

Master list

Everyone approved. Search by name, phone, plate or band, or tap a chip: Not in BMS, Not collected, Has a vehicle. Tap a person for Open pass and Copy phone. Export CSV for a printout.

Passes

Send each pass on WhatsApp and see what happened to it: Sent, Delivered, Read or Failed. A row’s Send asks once more before it goes. For a number outside India, open the pass and use Copy WhatsApp link.

Organisations

Each organisation, how many are approved and collected, and its contact. Tap one to see its people on the Master list.

Users and issuers

In the avatar menu, top right. To add an issuer:

  1. Invite operator. Their name, their real email, role Issuer, then Send invitation.

  2. They set a password from the email and sign in on their own phone, with signal, before their shift.

  3. One account per person, never shared. When someone is done, open them and tap Deactivate.
