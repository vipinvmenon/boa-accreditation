Design directions

Proposals only — nothing here is built except where marked SHIPPED. Pick one per surface (or mix: "L2 with the footer F1"). All use the real band art (placeholder crops until the masters arrive) and the app's own fonts and tokens.

Rule across every option: text never sits on artwork, no glow blobs, no floating pill tags. The band is treated as a physical object — a strip — not a wallpaper.

5 · Consistency fixes (no choice needed, for your OK)

  • Remove the corner glow blobs (the green/gold radial "orbs" behind every screen). They're the most template-looking thing in the app.
  • One green. The UI mint (#00F5A5) sits next to the AAA band green (#39E639) on passes and reads as an error. On the pass, the tier colour owns the page; mint stays for desk actions only.
  • Buttons and inputs from two shared components instead of 18 hand-combined class sets — this is where the "slightly different on every screen" feeling comes from.
  • One radius scale (28 / 22 / 14) applied everywhere; today some screens mix pill, 12px and 14px corners.
  • Copy: "crew" removed from messages sent to every tier; one-line fine print on the pass.
