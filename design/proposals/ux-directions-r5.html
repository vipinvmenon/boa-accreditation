Round 5: less

Round 4 stacked ten devices on one screen: glass, gold outline, notches, perforation, drop shadow, band strip, glare, mono labels, rule tags, help line. This round removes most of them. The poster gets its own space instead of hiding behind glass. The band is a thin woven edge, like the real wristband wrapped round the ticket. Labels become plain sentences. One accent colour per screen: the tier on the pass, mint on the sign-in button. Nothing else competes.

The pass

Art, band, who you are, where you can go, the code. Organisation and role read as a sentence. Zones are one quiet line with the zone-map colour as a small square; rules are words ("with ID", "after 10 PM"), not badges. Help lives in the WhatsApp chat the pass came from, so it isn't repeated here. Fits one screen inside WhatsApp, busiest case included.
